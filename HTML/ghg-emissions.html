Project Overview

GHG emissions benchmarking across industry sectors using NAICS codes.

This Excel dashboard analyzes greenhouse gas emissions data from Quantum Analytics NG, organized by NAICS industry classifications. The analysis reveals which sectors contribute most to carbon emissions, intensity by revenue, and relative environmental impact across manufacturing, energy, transportation, and service industries.

Sustainability managers, ESG analysts, and policymakers can use these insights to prioritize decarbonization initiatives, benchmark industry performance, set realistic reduction targets, and communicate environmental impact to stakeholders using standardized NAICS sector comparisons.

Data & Tools

Industry-level emissions data standardized by NAICS classification.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – Greenhouse gas emissions dataset mapped to NAICS industry codes across Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions.
  • Grain: Company or facility-level records with NAICS codes, total GHG emissions (tCO2e), revenue, employee count, and emission source breakdowns.
  • Excel features: Hierarchical NAICS slicers (2-digit → 6-digit), calculated emission intensity metrics, conditional formatting for high emitters, Pareto analysis for top contributors.

NAICS Slicer

Hierarchical industry classification filtering (2-6 digit NAICS).

Multi-level NAICS slicer enables granular sector analysis:

  • NAICS Codes: Filter from broad 2-digit sectors (Manufacturing=31-33, Energy=21) down to specific 6-digit industries (Apparel Manufacturing=3152).
  • Dynamic drill-down: Click through from economy-wide analysis to specific manufacturing subsectors or service categories.
  • Cross-industry comparison: Benchmark emissions intensity between similar industries or across economic sectors.

Enables questions like "Which manufacturing subsectors emit most per $ revenue?" or "How do service industries compare to heavy industry?"

Key Performance Indicators

Carbon emissions metrics standardized for industry benchmarking.

Total tCO2e
Greenhouse gas emissions for selected NAICS codes.
Emission Intensity
tCO2e per $1M revenue (industry standard).
Scope 1 %
Direct emissions as % of total emissions.
Carbon Productivity
$ revenue per tCO2e emitted.
NAICS Coverage
# of industries/sectors analyzed.
Top Emitters
# of industries contributing 80% emissions.

Excel measures with conditional formatting highlight worst/best performers by industry benchmark.

High-Emission Sectors

Industries driving disproportionate carbon impact.

  • Absolute emitters: Top NAICS sectors by total tCO2e (Utilities, Chemicals, Cement, Steel).
  • Intensity leaders: Highest emissions per $ revenue (carbon-intensive manufacturing).
  • Scope 3 impact: Industries with largest supply chain emissions footprint.

Identifies priority sectors for regulatory focus and decarbonization investment.

Low-Carbon Leaders

Industries demonstrating better environmental performance.

  • Efficiency champions: Service sectors, software, professional services with lowest intensity.
  • Progress indicators: Manufacturing subsectors showing emissions decoupling from revenue growth.
  • Benchmark targets: Realistic reduction goals based on top quartile performers.

Provides achievable targets for laggard industries and recognition for leaders.
