Project Overview

Sales performance by customer profile and product category using Excel.

This project uses a Retail Sales dataset from Quantum Analytics NG to analyze how revenue is distributed across age groups, gender, and product categories. The Excel dashboard brings together sales totals, average values, and product‑level metrics into a single interactive view using slicers and pivot tables.

The goal is to show how Excel can be used to build a lightweight but effective reporting solution that answers key questions about who is buying, what they buy, and how sales evolve over time.

Data & Tools

Transaction‑level sales data prepared for Excel reporting.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – Retail Sales dataset with customer, product, and transaction details.
  • Grain: Individual sales records including age category, gender, product category, price per unit, quantity, and date.
  • Preparation: Cleaned duplicates, standardized category labels, created age group bands, and ensured date fields were properly recognized for time‑based analysis.

Interactive Slicers

Filter the dashboard by time and product mix.

The Excel dashboard uses slicers connected to pivot tables to make exploration easy:

  • Year: Compare sales performance across years and understand growth over time.
  • Product Category: Focus on specific product groups to see how they contribute to total sales.

Combining these slicers lets users quickly answer questions such as “Which categories grew the most this year?” or “How did specific categories perform in earlier periods?”.

Key Performance Indicators

Core metrics that summarize quantity, sales value, and customer coverage.

Total Sales
Overall revenue generated from all retail transactions.
Total Quantity
Total units sold across all products and categories.
Total Products
Number of distinct products included in the dataset.
Age Categories
Coverage of different age groups involved in the sales.
Gender Mix
Sales split between different genders, useful for targeting.
Product Categories
Number of product categories represented in the analysis.

In Excel, these KPIs are implemented as pivot table aggregates and displayed as summary cards or highlighted cells at the top of the dashboard.

Customer & Sales Insights

How different customers contribute to sales.

  • Total sales by age category and gender: Shows which age and gender groups generate the most revenue, supporting targeted marketing and product decisions.
  • Average sales by age category and gender: Highlights typical spend per group to understand value per customer segment.
  • Total sales over the years: Tracks overall growth in sales and helps identify periods of acceleration or slowdown.

These views help clarify which customer segments are most valuable and how their behavior changes over time.

Product & Price Insights

What is selling, and at what price points.

  • Total sales by product category: Ranks categories by revenue to identify core lines and under‑performers.
  • Price per unit & quantity by product: Combines price and volume to show which products drive revenue through premium pricing or high volume.
  • Total sales by age and product category: Connects customer segments with specific product groups to reveal where preferences are strongest.

Together, these insights show where to focus inventory, pricing strategies, and promotional efforts.
