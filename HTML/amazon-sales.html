Project Overview

Understanding how customers interact with Amazon products across ratings, reviews, and pricing.

This project analyzes Amazon sales and product engagement data from Quantum Analytics NG. The dashboard is designed to answer questions such as which products attract the most attention, where average ratings are strongest, and how different price ranges relate to rating activity.

Using Power BI, the raw data is transformed into a clean model with interactive visuals that help stakeholders quickly see top products, highly rated items, and power users who leave the most reviews.

Data & Tools

Product‑level engagement data prepared for reporting.

  • Grain: Product‑level records with details such as product name, category, price, ratings, reviews, and user information.
  • Transformations: Cleaned missing or inconsistent rating values, standardized product and category names, and created calculated columns for price bands and engagement metrics.

Interactive Slicers

Fast, intuitive filtering for non‑technical users.

The report uses focused slicers to explore performance:

  • Product Name: Drill into a specific item to see its ratings, reviews, and engagement compared to others.
  • Category: Compare groups of products to understand which categories deliver the most value.

By combining these slicers, users can move from a high‑level view of all products to a narrow analysis of one product or one category in just a few clicks.

Key Performance Indicators

Metrics that summarize rating quality, engagement volume, and product coverage.

Average Rating
Overall average rating across all products in the selected context.
Max Rating
Highest rating observed for any product, useful for benchmarking top performers.
Total Rating
Sum of rating values across all rated products (e.g., stars aggregated).
Ratings Count
Total number of rating entries recorded in the dataset.
Total Products
Number of distinct products included in the analysis.
Total Reviews
Total number of review entries, showing the volume of written feedback.
Unique Users
Count of distinct users who interact through ratings or reviews, indicating how broad the engagement is.

In Power BI, these KPIs are implemented as measures and displayed as prominent cards at the top of the report.

Product & Rating Insights

Which products stand out, and how users respond.

  • Product by user engagement: Highlights products that generate the most interactions (ratings and reviews), showing what attracts customer attention.
  • Average ratings & total reviews by product: Combines rating quality with review volume to identify products that are both well‑rated and widely discussed.
  • Products with highest average ratings: Surfaces top‑rated products that may be candidates for promotion or recommendation.

These insights help distinguish products that are truly loved from those that receive attention but mixed feedback.

Price & User Behavior Insights

How pricing and users drive rating activity.

  • Total ratings by actual vs. discounted price: Examines how rating activity varies with the original price and discount levels.
  • Total ratings count by username: Identifies highly active reviewers who may act as power users or brand advocates.
  • Total products by actual price: Shows how product assortment is distributed across price ranges (e.g., budget vs. premium items).

These views allow business stakeholders to see where engagement concentrates and how pricing may influence customer behavior.
