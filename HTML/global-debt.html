Project Overview

Comprehensive global debt analysis across all sectors and countries.

This Power BI dashboard analyzes global debt data from Quantum Analytics NG, tracking Total Central Government Debt, Total Country coverage, Total General Government Debt, Total Household Debt, Total Non Financial Corporate Debt, and Total Private Debt across global economies.

Central banks, sovereign wealth funds, and macroeconomic analysts gain critical insights into debt sustainability, sector vulnerabilities, and cross-country risk comparisons for global portfolio management.

Comprehensive global debt dataset by country and sector.
