Project Overview

The TATA Online Retail dashboard summarizes multi‑year transaction data from Quantum Analytics NG, focusing on how sales behave across products, countries, and time. The goal is to help stakeholders quickly see top‑selling items, high‑value markets, and demand trends by month and quarter.

This project simulates a real retail analytics scenario: from raw order‑level data to a streamlined Power BI model with clear KPIs, reusable visuals, and slicers that support day‑to‑day commercial decisions.

Data & Modeling

Raw sales data transformed into a clean model ready for reporting.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – retail transaction data for TATA’s online store.
  • Granularity: Order‑level records including product details, customer, country, date, and quantity.
  • Transformations: Cleaned invalid entries, standardized product and country names, derived calendar fields (year, month, quarter), and created a star‑schema style model.

Interactive Slicers & Filters

Designed to let non‑technical users explore performance in seconds.

The report uses a set of slicers to filter the view:

  • Year: Compare performance across years and track growth over time.
  • Month: Spot seasonality and monthly peaks in demand.
  • Description (Product): Drill into a specific product’s sales behavior.
  • Country: Focus on a particular market or compare countries side by side.

These filters make the dashboard flexible enough for management, marketing, and operations teams, while keeping the layout simple and easy to read.

Key Performance Indicators

High‑level metrics that summarize customer volume, product breadth, demand, and revenue.

Total Customers
Distinct customers placing orders across all periods.
Total Order Count
Number of orders processed in the dataset.
Total Products
Unique products sold through the online channel.
Total Quantity
Total units sold across all products and periods.
Total Sales
Overall revenue generated from online retail transactions.

In your Power BI model, these KPIs are implemented as DAX measures and displayed as card visuals on the main page.

Product & Customer Insights

Which products drive value, and who buys them.

  • Top 5 sales by product: Identifies the products contributing the highest revenue, helping prioritize inventory and promotions.
  • Top 5 products by customer: Shows which items are most popular across the customer base, highlighting cross‑selling opportunities.
  • Top 5 products by quantity: Highlights high‑volume products that may have lower margins but drive traffic and repeat purchases.

Together, these insights help distinguish between revenue‑driving and volume‑driving products and guide assortment decisions.

Country & Time Insights

Where sales come from and how they evolve.

  • Total sales by country: Ranks countries by revenue to highlight key markets and underperforming regions.
  • Total sales by month and quarter: Reveals seasonal patterns and peak periods to support forecasting and campaign planning.
  • Total sales over the years: Shows long‑term growth (or decline), making it easy to see the overall trajectory of the online channel.

These views allow business users to quickly answer questions like “Which markets should we focus on?” and “When should we plan major campaigns?”.
