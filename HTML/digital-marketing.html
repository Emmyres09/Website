Project Overview

Campaign profitability analysis sliced by name and category.

This Power BI project analyzes digital marketing campaigns from Quantum Analytics NG, tracking Total Revenue, Gross Profit/Loss, Conversion 1, Conversion 2, Average Revenue, ROMI, CPL, CPC, CTR, AOV, and CAC. Slicers enable filtering by Campaign Name and Category.

Marketing teams gain actionable insights for budget allocation, campaign optimization, and ROI improvement across all digital channels and campaign types.

Data & Tools

Campaign-level performance data with spend and conversion metrics.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – Digital marketing dataset with campaign spend, conversions, revenue, and performance metrics.
  • Grain: Campaign-level records including impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue attribution, and cost data.
  • Power BI features: Slicers for Campaign Name and Category; DAX measures for ROMI, CPL, CPC, CTR, AOV, CAC calculations.

Slicer Setup

Interactive filtering by Campaign Name and Category.

Dual slicers enable precise analysis across individual campaigns and channel categories for comprehensive performance comparison.

  • Campaign Name: Filter specific campaigns for detailed performance review.
  • Category: Group by channel type (Search, Social, Display, Email, etc.).
  • Cross-analysis: Compare campaign performance within and across categories.

Enables questions like "Which Google Ads campaigns outperform Facebook?" or "How does Email category ROMI compare to Paid Search?"

Key Performance Indicators

Complete campaign profitability and efficiency metrics.

Total Revenue
Campaign-attributed revenue from selected filters.
Gross Profit/Loss
Revenue minus total campaign spend.
Conversion 1
Primary conversion count (leads, signups).
Conversion 2
Secondary conversion count (sales, purchases).
Average Revenue
Revenue per campaign in selection.
ROMI
Return on marketing investment ratio.
CPL
Cost per lead acquisition.
CPC
Cost per click efficiency.
CTR
Click-through rate performance.
AOV
Average order value by campaign.
CAC
Customer acquisition cost metric.

All 11 KPIs calculated using Power BI DAX measures with category benchmarks.

High-ROI Campaigns

Top performing campaigns by profitability metrics.

  • ROMI leaders: Campaigns exceeding 400% return targets.
  • Conversion drivers: Highest Conversion 1→2 rates.
  • Efficiency winners: Best CPL/CPC in high-volume categories.

Prioritize budget increases for proven high-ROI performers.

Optimization Opportunities

Underperforming campaigns needing attention.

  • Loss-making: Negative Gross Profit/Loss campaigns.
  • High CPL: Costly lead generation requiring targeting fixes.
  • Low CTR: Poor ad creative performance.

Focus optimization efforts on highest impact improvement areas.
