Project Overview

E-commerce performance by customer age, clothing division, and department.

This Excel dashboard analyzes women's clothing e-commerce sales data from Quantum Analytics NG, breaking down performance across age categories, clothing divisions (casual, formal, sportswear, etc.), and departments (tops, bottoms, dresses, accessories). Using pivot tables, slicers, and dynamic charts, the analysis reveals which customer segments drive sales in specific clothing categories.

Fashion retailers can use these insights to optimize inventory allocation, target marketing campaigns by age group, and develop product strategies that match customer preferences across different clothing segments.

Data & Tools

Transaction-level e-commerce data organized for fashion analytics.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – Women clothing e-commerce dataset with sales transactions, customer demographics, and product catalog.
  • Grain: Order-line level records including age category, division name (casualwear, formalwear, sportswear), department name (tops, dresses, bottoms), quantities, sales value, and dates.
  • Excel features: Multi-level pivot tables, interconnected slicers, conditional formatting for top/bottom performers, calculated fields for sales velocity and conversion rates.

Interactive Slicers

Filter by customer age, clothing division, and department.

Three interconnected slicers provide comprehensive filtering:

  • Age Category: Compare Gen Z, Millennials, Gen X, and Boomers to understand generational fashion preferences.
  • Division Name: Analyze performance across casualwear, formalwear, sportswear, intimates, and outerwear divisions.
  • Department Name: Drill into specific product types (tops, dresses, bottoms, outerwear, accessories, footwear).

Slicer combinations answer critical questions like "Do younger customers prefer sportswear tops while older customers favor dresses?"

Age & Category Insights

Generational preferences across clothing divisions.

  • Sales by age & division: Which generations drive sales in casualwear vs. formalwear vs. sportswear.
  • Department winners by age: Age group preferences for tops vs. dresses vs. bottoms vs. accessories.
  • Spend patterns: Average order values and purchase frequency by age category.

Reveals opportunities to tailor marketing and inventory by generation.

Division & Performance Insights

Which clothing categories perform across all ages.

  • Division rankings: Revenue leaders across casual, formal, sportswear, and intimates divisions.
  • Department cross-sell: Which combinations (tops + bottoms, dresses + accessories) drive highest AOV.
  • Seasonal patterns: Time-based trends in division/department performance by age group.

Identifies inventory priorities and merchandising opportunities.
