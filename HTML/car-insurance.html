Project Overview

Insurance claims patterns by vehicle, driver demographics, and coverage zones.

This Power BI dashboard analyzes car insurance claims data from Quantum Analytics NG to uncover patterns in claim frequency, claim amounts, and risk factors. The analysis connects vehicle characteristics (make, model, color, year) with driver demographics (education, gender, kids driving) and geographic factors (coverage zone).

The goal is to help insurance companies identify high‑risk combinations of cars, drivers, and locations for better underwriting, pricing, and risk management decisions.

Data & Tools

Policy and claims data transformed for insurance risk analysis.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – Car insurance policies and claims dataset.
  • Grain: Policy‑level records with vehicle details (make, model, year, color), driver info (gender, education, kids driving), household data, and claim history.
  • Transformations: Standardized vehicle names, created coverage zone groupings, calculated claim frequency rates, and built relationships between policy and claims tables.

Interactive Slicers

Vehicle and geographic drill‑down capabilities.

The dashboard provides comprehensive slicers for detailed analysis:

  • Car Make: Compare claim patterns across different manufacturers.
  • Car Model: Drill into specific models to identify high‑risk vehicles.
  • Car Year: Analyze how vehicle age correlates with claim frequency.
  • Coverage Zone: Examine geographic risk patterns and regional differences.

These slicers enable underwriters to quickly isolate combinations of vehicle type, model year, and location to assess risk exposure.

Key Performance Indicators

Core insurance metrics for claims volume, severity, and policyholder characteristics.

Household Income
Average income of policyholders filing claims.
Avg Claim Amount
Average cost per individual claim across selected filters.
Total Claim Amount
Total dollars paid out for all claims in the analysis.
Claim Frequency
Total number of claims filed across all policies.
Kids Driving
Total number of children listed as drivers on policies.

These KPIs are implemented as DAX measures and displayed as prominent cards at the top of the Power BI report.

Driver & Vehicle Insights

Risk patterns by demographics and vehicle characteristics.

  • Total kids driving by gender & car use: Identifies risky combinations of young drivers, gender, and vehicle usage patterns.
  • Total claim frequency by education: Examines whether driver education level correlates with accident rates.
  • Top car make & model by avg claim amount: Pinpoints specific vehicle combinations that generate highest repair costs.

These insights help underwriters adjust premiums based on actual risk patterns rather than assumptions.

Claims & Coverage Insights

Geographic and vehicle color risk patterns.

  • Total claim frequency & amount by car color: Surprising patterns emerge when analyzing claims by vehicle color.
  • Average claim amount by coverage zone: Reveals which geographic areas have highest claim severity.
  • Total claim amount & frequency by household: Connects household characteristics with claims behavior.

Coverage zone analysis is particularly valuable for regional pricing and risk pool management.
