Project Overview

End‑to‑end supply chain performance by product and customer segments.

This Excel dashboard analyzes supply chain operations data from Quantum Analytics NG, focusing on delivery performance, inventory efficiency, and customer satisfaction across different product types and demographic segments. Using advanced pivot tables, slicers, and dynamic charts, the dashboard reveals bottlenecks, high‑performing segments, and optimization opportunities.

The analysis helps supply chain managers identify which products move fastest through which customer groups, optimize inventory allocation, and improve on‑time delivery rates through data‑driven decisions.

Data & Tools

Operational data transformed for supply chain reporting.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – Supply chain operations dataset with orders, deliveries, inventory, and customer details.
  • Grain: Order‑line level records including product type, customer demographics, delivery dates, quantities, costs, and performance metrics.
  • Excel features: Pivot tables for aggregation, slicers for interactivity, conditional formatting for exceptions, calculated fields for KPIs like on‑time delivery % and fill rates.

Interactive Slicers

Filter supply chain performance by product and customer.

The dashboard uses Excel slicers connected across multiple pivot tables:

  • Product Type: Compare performance across product categories (electronics, apparel, perishables, etc.) to identify fast/slow movers.
  • Customer Demographic: Analyze delivery success and inventory efficiency by age group, location type, or purchase behavior segments.

Slicer combinations reveal critical insights like "Which customer segments experience delays with high‑value electronics?" or "Are perishables performing better for younger demographics?".

Key Performance Indicators

Essential supply chain efficiency and customer satisfaction metrics.

On-Time Delivery
% of orders delivered within promised timeframe.
Fill Rate
% of orders fulfilled completely from available stock.
Inventory Turnover
How quickly inventory converts to sales.
Avg Delivery Time
Average days from order to delivery.
Perfect Order %
% of orders delivered complete, on‑time, undamaged.
Customer Segments
Coverage across demographic segments analyzed.

KPIs implemented as Excel calculated fields and pivot table measures with conditional formatting for quick issue identification.

Product Performance Insights

Which products flow efficiently through the supply chain.

  • Delivery performance by product type: Identifies which categories consistently meet SLAs vs. chronic delays.
  • Inventory efficiency by product: Shows stock turnover rates and fill rates by product category to optimize carrying costs.
  • Cost per product type: Compares transportation, warehousing, and handling costs across product categories.

These insights guide inventory planning, carrier selection, and product prioritization decisions.

Customer Segment Insights

Service levels by demographic and buying behavior.

  • Performance by customer demographic: Reveals which segments experience highest service levels vs. most delays.
  • Product‑demographic fit: Shows which product types perform best for different customer groups (age, location, value).
  • Priority customer analysis: Identifies high‑value segments needing premium service levels.

Enables targeted service improvements and capacity allocation by customer importance.
