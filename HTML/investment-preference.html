Project Overview

Investor behavior analysis by portfolio strategy and demographics.

This Power BI dashboard analyzes investment preference data from Quantum Analytics NG to uncover how different investor profiles choose their investment vehicles. The analysis explores preferences across investment monitors (mutual funds, stocks, bonds, crypto, etc.) and reveals significant gender-based patterns in risk appetite and asset allocation.

Financial advisors and wealth managers can use these insights to better understand client segmentation, tailor portfolio recommendations, and develop targeted investment products that match investor psychology and demographic trends.

Data & Tools

Investor profile and portfolio allocation data for behavioral analysis.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – Investment preference dataset capturing investor demographics, portfolio allocations, and investment monitor choices.
  • Grain: Investor‑level records with investment monitor preferences (mutual funds, ETFs, individual stocks, bonds, alternatives), allocation percentages, gender, age, and risk tolerance.
  • Transformations: Created investment monitor categories, calculated portfolio concentration ratios, built gender-based comparison measures, and established relationships for cross-filtering.

Interactive Slicers

Filter by investment strategy and investor demographics.

Strategic slicers enable deep behavioral analysis:

  • Invest Monitor: Filter by specific investment vehicles (Mutual Funds, ETFs, Stocks, Bonds, Crypto, Real Estate, Alternatives) to see adoption patterns and preferences.
  • Gender: Compare male vs. female investor behavior across all metrics to identify significant preference differences.

These slicers reveal powerful insights like "Do women prefer income-focused mutual funds while men chase growth stocks?" or "Which alternative assets attract both genders equally?"

Investment Monitor Insights

Which investment vehicles attract which investors.

  • Monitor adoption rates: Market share of each investment type (mutual funds vs. ETFs vs. individual stocks vs. crypto) across the investor population.
  • Concentration patterns: How much investors concentrate in their preferred monitor vs. diversification across multiple types.
  • Emerging preferences: Growth rates of newer investment monitors (crypto, alternatives) vs. traditional choices.

Reveals whether investors are becoming more specialized or broadly diversified in their approach.

Gender Behavioral Insights

Significant differences in risk appetite and style.

  • Gender preference gaps: Statistically significant differences in monitor choice between male and female investors.
  • Risk tolerance by gender: Comparative analysis of conservative (bonds, mutual funds) vs. aggressive (stocks, crypto) preferences.
  • Portfolio construction: Average number of monitors and concentration ratios by gender.

Critical insights for financial advisors segmenting clients by behavioral patterns rather than just demographics.
