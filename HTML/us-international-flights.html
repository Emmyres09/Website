Project Overview

U.S. international flight operations analysis by carrier and time period.

This Power BI dashboard analyzes U.S. international flight data from Quantum Analytics NG, tracking Total Carriers, Total FG Airports, Total Flights, and Total Usage Airports. Triple slicers (Year, Month, Carrier) enable granular network analysis.

Airlines, airports, and aviation authorities gain insights into carrier network expansion, seasonal patterns, and international connectivity across U.S. gateway airports.

Data & Tools

U.S. international flight operations dataset.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – U.S. international flight records with carrier, origin/destination airports, dates, and operational metrics.
  • Grain: Flight-level records including carrier code, FG (Foreign Gateway) airports, usage airports, and operational dates.
  • Power BI features: Year/Month/Carrier slicers, DAX measures for network metrics, airport mapping, and time intelligence analysis.

Slicer Setup

Year, Month, and Carrier filtering controls.

Triple slicers provide comprehensive temporal and carrier analysis across U.S. international operations.

  • Year: Multi-year trend analysis of international capacity.
  • Month: Seasonal patterns and peak travel periods.
  • Carrier: Individual airline performance (American, Delta, United, foreign carriers).

Enables questions like "Which carriers grew fastest in summer 2024?" or "JFK vs MIA international traffic by carrier?"
