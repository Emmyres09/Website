Project Overview

Global prevalence analysis of 8 major mental health disorders.

This Power BI dashboard analyzes global mental health data from Quantum Analytics NG, tracking Average Alcohol Use, Anxiety Disorder, Bipolar Disorder, Depression, Drug Use Disorder, Eating Disorder, Schizophrenia prevalence rates plus Total Female, Total Male, and Total Population affected worldwide.

Public health officials, policymakers, and healthcare providers gain critical insights into disorder prevalence patterns, gender disparities, and population-level mental health burden for resource allocation and intervention planning.

Data & Tools

Comprehensive global mental health prevalence dataset.

  • Data source: Quantum Analytics NG – Global mental health dataset covering 8 major disorders across countries with gender and age breakdowns.
  • Grain: Country-disorder-gender records showing prevalence rates, case counts, and population demographics.
  • Power BI features: Disorder slicers, gender analysis, heatmaps, trend lines, population pyramids, and DAX prevalence calculations.

Disorder Coverage

Complete analysis of 8 major mental health conditions.

Full spectrum coverage of prevalent mental disorders enables comprehensive burden-of-disease analysis and resource planning.

  • 7 Clinical Disorders: Alcohol Use, Anxiety, Bipolar, Depression, Drug Use, Eating Disorders, Schizophrenia.
  • Demographic Splits: Total Female, Total Male, Total Population affected globally.
  • Prevalence Metrics: Average rates per 100,000 population standardized globally.

Benchmark depression (5% global prevalence) vs schizophrenia (0.3%) or analyze female vs male anxiety gaps.

Prevalence Leaders

Disorders with highest global population impact.

  • Anxiety & Depression: Leading disorders affecting 300M+ globally each.
  • Gender disparity: Females 1.5-2x higher rates across most disorders.
  • Substance impact: Alcohol/drug disorders in top 5 prevalence globally.

Highest priority disorders for public health intervention.

Underserved Conditions

Lower prevalence but high treatment gap disorders.

  • Schizophrenia/Bipolar: Lower prevalence but devastating impact.
  • Eating disorders: Significant female burden, under-resourced globally.
  • Male gaps: Underreported substance disorders and suicide risk.

Critical areas for specialized mental health services.
