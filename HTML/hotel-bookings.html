Project Overview

ADR and stay metrics by customer type and deposit type with arrival-date slicers.

This Power BI project analyzes hotel bookings data from Quantum Analytics NG, focusing on Average Daily Rate (ADR), Average Nights, Total Customers, and Lead Time. Slicers enable segmentation by Customer Type, Deposit Type, Arrival Month, and Arrival Year for granular insights.

Revenue optimization, capacity planning, and demand forecasting are enabled for hotels, chains, and management companies. The visuals support scenario analysis and executive storytelling with clear KPI indicators.

Slicer Setup

Interactive filtering by key booking dimensions.

The slicers allow you to compare performance across customer types (e.g., Corporate, OTA, Walk-in) and deposit types (non-refundable, refundable), and to analyze trends by month and year of arrival.

  • Customer Type: Filter by segments such as Corporate, LE, OTA, and Group.
  • Deposit Type: Distinguish refundable vs non-refundable bookings.
  • Arrival Month/Year: Time-based slicing for seasonal analysis.

This setup supports questions like "Which customer type drives ADR growth in peak season?" or "Do refundable bookings have longer lead times?"

Key Performance Indicators

Booking performance metrics for revenue optimization.

Total ADR
Average daily rate across filtered bookings.
Average Nights
Average stay duration per booking.
Total Customers
Count of unique guests in the period.
Total Lead Time
Average days between booking date and arrival date.
Occupancy Insights
Capacity utilization trend by segment.
Deposit Mix
Share of refundable vs non-refundable bookings.

KPIs are computed in Power BI using measures and a date table for consistent time intelligence.

High-Impact Segments

Segments driving mixed performance.

  • ADR leaders: Corporate bookings with higher ADR during peak months.
  • Nights drivers: Groups and long-stay segments contributing to extended stays.
  • Lead time patterns: Non-refundable deposits often show shorter lead times.

Insights help tailor rate strategies and promotional offers to optimize revenue.

Actionable Takeaways

Practical strategies derived from the analysis.

  • Dynamic pricing: Adjust ADR by Customer Type and Deposit Type during high-demand months.
  • Promotions: Promote refundable bookings in off-peak months to balance occupancy.
  • Lead-time optimization: Align marketing campaigns with expected lead-time windows per segment.

Use these takeaways to inform pricing, inventory controls, and marketing calendars.
